## Almond Flour Chocolate Chip Cookies 

# **Ingredients** 

- 7 tbsp softened butter 

- 2/3 cup light brown sugar 

- 1 large egg 

- 2 tsp vanilla 

- 2 cups almond flour *not almond meal* 

- 1 tbsp corn starch 

- 1/2 tsp baking soda 

- 1/2 tsp sea salt 

- 2/3 cup semi sweet chocolate chips 

# **Instructions** 

- Add butter to mixing bowl/stand mixer (if not already softened, microwave for about 15 seconds). 

- Add in sugar and beat with butter until well combined, about 30 seconds. 

- Beat in egg and vanilla until egg is completely broken up. 

- Refrigerate dough for 20 min - *DO NOT SKIP*. 

- Preheat oven to 350 degrees. 

- When cookie dough has chilled, scoop onto cookie sheet in roughly 1 tbsp balls and do not flatten. 

- Bake 13-18 minutes. 

- Remove from oven and let cool.
